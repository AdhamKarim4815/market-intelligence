What this does not claim

The attention score orders this inbox by relevance to your positions. It was measured against subsequent benchmark-relative returns over 88 events and does not predict them — Spearman +0.04 at one day, −0.40 at five. It is kept for ordering, not for forecasting, and the severity bands sit above the range it actually produces. The workings are in docs/CALIBRATION.md.
